05 How Ownership Works Here

You own the home. You lease the ground.

Buying a manufactured home in a community is different from buying a house with land. Here are the four things that shape this purchase.

The home

Personal property, with a title.

MLS: Mobile/Manufactured w/o Land · titled manufactured home

The home transfers by its title, much like a vehicle, rather than by a deed. Your closing provider confirms the title is clear of liens, that the serial and HUD numbers match the home, and that taxes are current. Colorado counties generally require a certificate showing taxes are paid before a manufactured home title can transfer. Your closing provider can confirm what is needed.

The land

A lease with the park.

MLS: Lease required · assign lease: No · lot rent: contact listing broker

You sign your own new lease with park management. The seller's lease does not transfer to you. Ask for the current lot rent, what it includes (for example water, sewer or trash), the lease length, the park rules, and the history of rent increases.

Colorado's Mobile Home Park Act gives home owners specific protections. For example, lot rent can rise only once in any 12-month period, and only with at least 60 days' written notice. The Colorado Division of Housing publishes a plain-language summary of home owner rights.

Approval

The park says yes before you close.

MLS: Buyer must be approved by park management prior to close

Every offer needs to include an approved park application. That is unusual, so start early. Ask park management for the application, its fee, its criteria and how long a decision takes. Ask about pets, vehicles, occupancy, and any community age requirements, and get the park rules in writing.

Paying for it

Cash or a chattel loan.

MLS: Acceptable financing: cash, chattel

A chattel loan is financing secured by the home itself, not by land. Terms usually differ from a standard mortgage, and not every lender offers one. Offers must include proof of funds. If you will finance, talk to a lender that makes manufactured-home chattel loans before you write an offer. See the lender directory to start.

The order matters. Your financing (or proof of funds) and an approved park application both need to be in hand to submit an offer here. Starting both this week puts you in position to act, especially after a price reduction.
